# PDP & ISO 27001 Compliance Map 🗺️

This document maps how the technical challenges in **PDP Koans (Django Edition)** directly align with the legal compliance requirements of **Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP)** and **ISO 27001 security controls**.

---

## 📋 Compliance Coverage Table

| Koan Challenge | Legal Aspect (UU PDP) | Standard Control (ISO 27001) | Technical Implementation Topic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Koan 01: Data Minimisation**<br>`koans/k01_data_minimization/` | **Article 16 UU PDP**:<br>Limits of data processing | **Control A.8.11**:<br>Data masking & access restriction | **Data Minimisation & Masking**:<br>Removing irrelevant database fields to reduce exposure risks, and implementing a property method to mask sensitive data (e.g. phone numbers) before logging or rendering. |
| **Koan 02: Explicit Consent**<br>`koans/k02_explicit_consent/` | **Articles 20 & 21 UU PDP**:<br>Written/recorded consent requirement | **Control A.5.15**:<br>Access control & consent management | **Processing Foundations & Proof of Consent**:<br>Validating user consent prior to data processing, and recording audit logs (Consent Logs) with IP address, timestamp, and policy version to prove compliance under Article 21. |
| **Koan 03: Data Security**<br>`koans/k03_data_security/` | **Article 39 UU PDP**:<br>Duty of data security preservation | **Control A.8.24**:<br>Use of cryptography (Encryption *at-rest*) | **Data Security & Cryptography**:<br>Translating ISO 27001 cryptography controls into code by building a custom *EncryptedCharField* using AES (Fernet) to transparently encrypt sensitive data (NIK) at-rest in PostgreSQL. |
| **Koan 04: RBAC & Access Audit Trail**<br>`koans/k04_rbac_audit/` | **Article 40 UU PDP**:<br>Restricted access control rules | **Control A.8.2 / A.5.15**:<br>Access rights & audit logging | **Role-Based Access Control & Audit Trails**:<br>Restricting access to DPO roles using a custom permission class, and automatically logging access details (operator email, customer ID, IP address) for audit compliance. |
| **Koan 05: Data Portability**<br>`koans/k05_data_portability/` | **Articles 7 & 13 UU PDP**:<br>Data subject rights to portability | **Control A.5.15**:<br>Data subject requests & access control | **Data Portability & IDOR Protection**:<br>Enabling users to export all personal records securely in structured JSON, and enforcing strict user-context checks to prevent BOLA/IDOR vulnerabilities. |
| **Koan 06: Data Breach Response**<br>`koans/k06_breach_response/` | **Article 35 UU PDP**:<br>Data breach notifications | **Control A.5.24**:<br>Information security incident management | **Breach Containment & BPPA Reporting**:<br>Detecting and isolating compromised accounts (containment), and automatically compiling standard BPPA-compliant incident notification reports. |
| **Koan 07: Data Deletion & Anonymisation**<br>`koans/k07_deletion_anonymisation/` | **Articles 16 & 43 UU PDP**:<br>Right to erasure / Right to be forgotten | **Control A.8.10 / A.8.14**:<br>Information deletion & redundancy redundancy management | **Erasure vs Anonymisation**:<br>Implementing an API that hard-deletes directly identifying records (User, Profile, Consent Logs) while anonymizing transaction history (matching emails replaced with pseudonymous values) to satisfy legal request compliance while retaining business analytical capabilities. |
| **Koan 08: Consent Withdrawal**<br>`koans/k08_consent_withdrawal/` | **Articles 15 & 40 UU PDP**:<br>Right to withdraw consent | **Control A.5.15**:<br>Access control & consent management | **Consent Revocation Flow**:<br>Implementing an endpoint that records the consent withdrawal audit event (`consent_given = False`) and immediately deactivates the user login credentials (`is_active = False`) to prevent active processing of personal data, while keeping historical data for compliance (tax, reporting) until the retention period ends. |
| **Koan 09: Purpose Limitation**<br>`koans/k09_purpose_limitation/` | **Articles 16 & 27 UU PDP**:<br>Limitation of data processing purpose | **Control A.5.15**:<br>Access control & consent verification | **Data Purpose Separation**:<br>Enforcing separation of processing purposes by restricting marketing newsletter dispatches only to users who explicitly opted-in to promotional communications (`marketing_consent = True`), preventing unauthorized secondary processing. |
| **Koan 10: Data Retention Policy**<br>`koans/k10_data_retention/` | **Articles 16 & 43 UU PDP**:<br>Deletion of personal data / Retentions | **Control A.8.10**:<br>Information deletion | **Automated Data Purging**:<br>Implementing a Django custom management command (`purge_expired_logs`) to automatically delete system/audit logs that exceed the designated retention threshold period (e.g. older than X days), preventing indefinite storage of personal logs. |
